Hoof Picks Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Hoof Picks ("the App") is a novelty random-number utility for entertainment purposes only. It is not a gambling app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Powerball, Mega Millions, any state lottery, or their operators. This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Data We Collect

None. Hoof Picks does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The App does not require an account, sign-in, email address, or any personal information to function.

Data Stored On Your Device

If you tap "Save Pick," the App stores your generated numbers locally on your device using standard on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This data:

  • Never leaves your device or gets sent to us or any third party
  • Is not linked to your identity
  • Can be deleted at any time using the "Clear all" option in the App, or by deleting the App

Third-Party Services

Hoof Picks does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or any third-party SDKs that collect data.

Sharing

If you use the "Share" feature, the App uses your device's built-in share sheet to let you send a text message through apps you already have installed (e.g., Messages, Mail). We do not have access to, or receive a copy of, anything you share.

Children's Privacy

Hoof Picks does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hoofpicksappsupport@gmail.com.
