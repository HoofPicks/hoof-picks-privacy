Hoof Picks is a novelty random-number utility for entertainment purposes only. It is not a gambling app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any lottery, state lottery, or their operators.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate numbers?
Open 5 Numbers & Bonus for a quick-pick style format, or Random Draw to set your own range and count.

Where are my saved picks stored?
Saved picks stay on your device only. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

How do I delete my saved picks?
Go to My Numbers and tap "Clear all," or delete individual picks with "Remove." Uninstalling the app also removes all saved data.

Does Hoof Picks sell lottery tickets or accept wagers?
No. Hoof Picks does not sell tickets, accept wagers, or guarantee winnings of any kind. It is for entertainment only.
